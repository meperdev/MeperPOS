## Decisions (orchestrator)

- D1 (S3) Payment rule, enforced in `SalesService.create` and mirrored in the POS for UX: overpay = paid - total, rounded to cents. If overpay > 0, a CASH payment must exist. On a positive total, every payment amount must be strictly greater than the overpay (removing any one must leave the total uncovered). On a zero total, only cash may be handed back (preserves SEC-015 fully discounted sales). Change = overpay (not cashPaid - total).
- D2 (S1) Warm "sand" ink replaces neon success/info in charts; Salidas keeps a softened danger.
- D3 (S6) The route exists in code (commit 0769463, on origin/master); a 404 "Cannot POST" means the deployed backend predates it. `canSend` already hides the button once `sentAt` exists; it stayed because the send never succeeded.
- D4 (S8) The link is not cosmetic: `financial-aggregator.ts:136` excludes expenses with `purchaseOrderId` from operating expenses (they are merchandise cost). Linking an unrelated expense silently removes it from the P&L. Rule: the purchase order selector only lists orders of the chosen supplier in PENDING, PARTIAL_RECEIVED or RECEIVED; choosing an order fills the supplier; the backend rejects an order from another supplier or in DRAFT/CANCELLED (validated only when the order or supplier changes, so legacy rows stay editable). The form explains the consequence; the detail shows the order as a linked summary.
- D5 (S9) Summary becomes a hero total (month in words, group count, one proportional stacked bar) plus a ranked group list (amount, share, top labels) using categorical chart tokens.
