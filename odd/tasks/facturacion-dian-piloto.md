- S8: "Documento fiscal + máquina de estados mínima"; "Emitir siempre al registrar la venta". A fiscal document row per sale, isolated by organization, with the states PENDING, SENT, ACCEPTED, REJECTED and VOIDED; an invalid transition answers 409 instead of silence, and the fiscal snapshot is immutable. Credit and cash sales emit at sale registration, so the payment state never changes the fiscal moment. Credit notes are explicitly out of this task.
